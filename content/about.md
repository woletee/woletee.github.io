I read research papers and write them up the way I wish they had been explained to
me the first time: the question before the notation, a picture wherever a picture
helps, and code short enough to read in one sitting.

The main project here is a growing **[library of diffusion model
papers](/diffusion/)** — starting with the 2020 paper that made the whole
approach work, and following the chain of ideas forward to the systems behind
today's image and video generators.

Two rules I try to hold myself to:

1. **No hand-waving.** If I cannot explain why a step is there, I say so rather
   than papering over it.
2. **Say what the paper got wrong.** The limitations are the interesting part —
   they are what the next paper is about.

Corrections, arguments, and requests for papers to cover are all welcome:
[open an issue](https://github.com/woletee/woletee.github.io/issues).

Elsewhere: [GitHub](https://github.com/woletee).
